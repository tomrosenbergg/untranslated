Dream Interpretation

Dream interpretation is a powerful method for understanding the unconscious mind. Dreams often appear enigmatic, confusing or meaningless, but through interpretation they can reveal deeply held beliefs, fantasies and desires.

In our one-on-one sessions you will freely associate to the elements of your dream, saying whatever comes to mind without censorship. I will listen attentively, without judgement, and help you unravel the patterns of your unconscious thought. For many, the practice is a powerful method to overcome creative blocks and find a deeper self-understanding.
