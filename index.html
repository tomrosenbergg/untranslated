Dream Interpretation

Your dreams don't come with a dictionary. They speak in a language unique to you.

In my practice, I do not tell you what your dreams mean. Instead, I help you interpret the contents of your own mind. Using the technique of free association, we collaborate one-on-one to untangle the patterns of your unconscious thought. For many, dream interpretation is a way to overcome creative blocks and gain a deeper self-understanding. Once learned, the practice can be continued alone or with a partner.
